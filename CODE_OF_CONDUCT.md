# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, safe, and constructive community for people
of every background, identity, experience level, and ability. Participation in
project spaces requires respect, patience, and good faith.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence without attacking people.
- Give and receive feedback with empathy and professional care.
- Respect privacy, consent, boundaries, and differing levels of expertise.
- Credit the work of others and disclose relevant conflicts of interest.
- Help make technical discussions accessible and focused.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, sustained disruption, and retaliation are not
acceptable. This policy applies in repository discussions, reviews, community
events, and private communications undertaken on behalf of the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

If it is safe to do so, participants may ask someone to stop inappropriate
behavior. Private reports can be sent to
[security@categori.se](mailto:security@categori.se) with “Code of Conduct” in
the subject. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical; conflicts
of interest will be avoided when selecting reviewers.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue a private warning, temporarily
limit participation, or permanently exclude a participant. Responses will be
proportionate to the behavior, its impact, prior incidents, and the safety of
the community. Good-faith reports and appeals will not be used as grounds for
retaliation.
